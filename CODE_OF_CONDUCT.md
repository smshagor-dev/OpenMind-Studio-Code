# Code of conduct

OpenMind Studio's community — issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space — is open to everyone.
We expect everyone taking part to:

- be respectful and considerate, and assume good intent;
- criticise ideas and code, never people;
- accept constructive feedback, and give it the same way;
- respect people's time, privacy and differing experience.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults or discriminatory remarks, personal attacks, publishing others' private
information, sexualised language or imagery, and sustained disruption.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break these rules and may block people who keep breaking them.
Report a problem to smshagor.dev@gmail.com; reports are handled confidentially.
